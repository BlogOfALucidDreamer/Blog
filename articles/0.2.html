How to have lucid dreams ?

In this article, we will seeHow to have lucid dreams ? There are a lot of techniques and mindsets that will help you to have lucid dreams. In this article, we will see some of them, but we will explore those techniques and mindsets more in details in the future.

Keeping a dream journal

The very first step is to keep a dream journal. Keeping a dream journal will enable you to remember your dreams better. Because if you can't remember your dreams, you won't be able to remember your lucid ones, so keeping a dream journal is very important. If you're a beginner, it's totally normal to struggle with your memory of your dream, but if you manage to write down some, your brain will be like "oh he/she is trying to remember his/her dreams, I will try to remember my dreams better !" It's the first thing you need to do if you want to experience lucid dreams.
So take a little notebook you can access once you wake up and write your dreams every morning when you have the best memory of your dream. When you write your dream, write the date first, and remember to add as many as details as possible, and not just the images that comes to you, but your emotions, sensations, sounds you've heard, thoughts you had. Even if you can't remember anything, write in your journal that you don’t remember your dream from last night.
This dream journal will be very useful because every night, just before you go to sleep, you should read your previous dream. So grab your notebook tonight and write down whatever you remember from your last dream — even tiny fragments count !

The reality checks

A reality check is a second very important thing you need to learn before jumping into the techniques to become lucid. A reality check is a small reflex or technique that allows you to know if you're in a dream or in reality, if you've seen the film Inception, it's like their totems but in more practical and if you havn't seen it yet, I strongly recommend it. Like I already said in my previous article, the dreams seem just like real life but there are a few details that changes in dreams. That's why you need to learn what these details are.
There are a lot of reality checks.

To know if you're in a dream or in reality, you can :

  • Look at your hands because in a dream they will look weird or you will not have more or less fingers.
  • Pinch your nose and try to breathe through it because in a dream you will be able to breath through your nose.
  • Look at a text two times and see if it has changed.
  • Look at a clock Two times and see if the time is the same.

The different techniques

Here are the different techniques that works and their strengths and weaknesses:

Mild

The Mild (Mnemonic Initiated lucid dream) technique involves setting an intention before sleep to become lucid in your dreams. This can be done by repeating a phrase like "I will become lucid in my dreams" or visualizing yourself as a lucid dreamer.

Wbtb

The Wbtb (Wake Back To Bed) technique involves waking up after 4-6 hours of sleep, staying awake for a short period of time, and then going back to sleep with the intention of having a lucid dream. This technique takes advantage of the fact that REM sleep, where most vivid dreams occur, is more likely to happen in the later part of the night.

Wild

The Wild (Wake Initiated Lucid Dream) technique involves staying conscious while your body falls asleep. This can be achieved through relaxation techniques, meditation, or visualization. The goal is to enter a lucid dream directly from a waking state.

Fild

The Fild (Finger Induced Lucid Dream) technique involves using small finger movements to trigger a lucid dream. This can be done by lightly tapping your fingers together or against a surface while falling asleep. The idea is that the sensation of the finger movements can help you become aware that you're dreaming.

Dild

The Dild (Dream Induced Lucid Dream) technique involves using a dream sign or trigger to become lucid. This can be a specific object, person, or situation that you associate with dreaming. When you encounter this trigger in your dream, it can help you realize that you're dreaming and become lucid.
