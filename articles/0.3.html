The risks of lucid dreaming

In this article, we will seeThe risks of lucid dreaming because we can't dive directly into this amazing lucid adventure without knowing what the risks are.

Confusion between dreams and reality

If you've already read the precedent article

, you know that the whole point of lucid dreaming is about knowing whenever you are in a dream. And to do so, you will need to ask yourself questions about tghe reality of your world. If you're a beginner, you hould have non problem about this, but if you're an advanced lucid dreamer that become lucid easily and often, then you might whip your dream memories and your real memories. This type of mistake can leads you to some funny interactions with peoples you thought you said something but can be very destabilizing.

Bad sleep habits

Some lucid dreaming techniques like the Wbtb technique imply that you wake up very early on the night to go back to bed. That can be really tiring. The simple fact of having a lucid dream means that you're disrupting your REM phase, so lucid dreaming can sometimes become very tiring because of bad sleep. You need to understand that you will not have any lucid dream once you're too tired, so if you often have lucid dream, think to take breaks to sleep some nights.
